Omelette

To make an omelette, follow these steps (you can swap or add ingredients as per your taste).

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Bread crumbs
  • Butter, ghee or cooking oil
  • Green onion
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Take the eggs out.
  2. Break the eggs and pour them into a bowl.
  3. Add a little bread crumb as per your liking.
  4. Add salt and green onion.
  5. Heat the pan on the stove.
  6. After 1 minute, add butter, ghee or cooking oil to the pan.
  7. Let it heat for 1-2 minutes.
  8. Pour the egg batter into the pan.
  9. Flip the egg to the other side after 2-3 minutes.
  10. You can flip it for a round whole omelette, or scramble it if you prefer.
  11. Finally, turn off the stove and plate the omelette in your bowl.
